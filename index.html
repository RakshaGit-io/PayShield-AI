How it works

One decision per session, made before the payment is attempted.

1

Telemetry arrives

The checkout sends attempt count, timing, device, cart changes and request speed.

2

The classifier decides

It returns rescue, block or allow, a confidence score and one sentence of reasoning.

3

The route follows

Rescued customers get a PayPal order. Bots are stopped. Normal buyers pass through.
