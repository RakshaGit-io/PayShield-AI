PayShield AI
Block the Bots. Keep the Ka-Ching! 
    PayShield AI is an AI-assisted payment protection concept designed to help merchants distinguish genuine customer payment retries from suspicious automated bot activity.
Every genuine customer matters. A legitimate payment retry shouldn't automatically become a lost sale.

The Problem
   Imagine standing at a coffee counter, trying to pay for your coffee. Your payment fails, so you try again. But instead of completing your purchase, the system flags your repeated attempts as suspicious activity.
That's a frustrating experience for customers — and lost revenue for merchants.
  Traditional fraud prevention can sometimes struggle to distinguish genuine customer behaviour from automated attacks.

 My Solution
   PayShield AI demonstrates a smarter approach to payment protection by examining checkout behaviour and contextual signals to help distinguish legitimate retries from suspicious activity.
Instead of treating every repeated request as a threat, it aims to make more informed decisions.

 Key Features
Split-Screen Checkout Demo: Compare a traditional checkout experience with PayShield AI enabled.

Customer Retry Simulation: Demonstrate how legitimate payment retries can be treated differently.

Bot Attack Simulation: Trigger rapid automated requests to demonstrate suspicious activity detection.

Context-Aware Classification: Use behavioural signals and request patterns to classify checkout activity.

Live Decision Dashboard: Display payment protection activity and key performance indicators.

Revenue Protection Concept: Demonstrate how preventing unnecessary declines could help merchants retain genuine transactions.

 How It Works
  A customer initiates a checkout attempt.
     PayShield AI evaluates available behavioural and contextual signals.
The system classifies the activity as potentially genuine or suspicious.
The dashboard displays the resulting decision.
Users can compare the traditional checkout experience with the PayShield AI-enabled experience.

Demo Disclaimer
PayShield AI is a hackathon prototype.
The checkout and payment scenarios are simulated demonstrations. The current demo does not process real payments or create actual PayPal transactions.
AI-assisted classification and fallback logic demonstrate the proposed decision-making approach; production payment integrations and real-world fraud detection would require further development and testing.
